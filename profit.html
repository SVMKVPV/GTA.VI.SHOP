Enter verified Australian-dollar supplier costs. Estimates are not guarantees. No supplier API or checkout is connected. This calculator runs locally in your browser; do not use it to publish unverified listings.

Enter supplier item and shipping costs to calculate.

Formula: price = (supplier + shipping + other + advertising + fixed payment fee) ÷ (1 − target margin − payment fee rate − refund allowance rate − GST fraction). GST fraction is GST rate / (100 + GST rate), assuming GST applies to the sale and no input tax credits are claimed in this conservative estimate. Validate your actual tax position and fees before using. Fixed overhead, income tax, exchange-rate changes and unusual chargebacks may further reduce profits.
